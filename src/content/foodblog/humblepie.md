Humble Pie definitely offers one of the best sweet-savory culinary experiences in Berlin. 
Their main attraction, the fried chicken sandwich, can be ordered with three different types of carbohydrates: the brioche, the biscuit, and the waffle.
You can couple these sandwiches up with a variety of sides such as: fries, mac&cheese, corn bread, and so many more delicious tiny bites.

They even have 5 different spice levels for the chicken, and although I am quite good at tolerating spice the fourth level wiped me out. 
Therefore I will probably be sticking with level 2 or 3 from now on, and I recommend you begin with the same.
The chicken itself is packed with flavor, and I would highly recommend you pick either the waffle or the brioche.
The waffle will give you an excellent sweet-savory experience, 
whereas the brioche will give you that standard fried chicken sandwich flavor you'd expect.
