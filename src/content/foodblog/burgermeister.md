I am no stranger to Burgers and neither is Burgermeister. Their burgers are the best bang for your buck on the Berlin Burger market. Most Burger places in Berlin are either too expensove, overhyped, or unreliable. Burgermeister delivers the same delciious burger every time for an unwavering affordable price.

Burgermeister burgers are smashed and served on the softest brioche buns one can find, coupled with sauces, cheese, and veggies. Their fries are nothing special, and I do not recommend them. For fries I would go to Five Guys and order the Cajun Fries, because those are in a league of their own. The place at Zoologischer Garten is my favorite because it is the best out of all in my opinion (yes the flavor is different based on where you are), and it also happens to be right next to my house. Burgermeister is also open till very late, and you usually don't need to wait long.
